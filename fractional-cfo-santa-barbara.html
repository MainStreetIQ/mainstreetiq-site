The Challenge

Why Santa Barbara Companies Outgrow Their Finance Function

The same qualities that make Santa Barbara County a great place to build a business, owner-operated companies in demanding verticals with seasonal demand and fast growth, also create financial complexity that spreadsheets and part-time bookkeepers cannot handle. These are the four verticals we know deepest.

Wine & DTC Club Economics

Your 2023 vintage looks great on paper. Club sign-ups are up. Tasting room revenue is tracking. So why is the line of credit maxed and harvest is six weeks out? Because your CPA is not modeling the cash conversion cycle and your bookkeeper is not flagging it. Wineries across Santa Ynez, Solvang, Buellton, and Lompoc hit this wall almost every year. A fractional CFO builds the 13-week cash forecast that tells you in June whether you will make it to crush, and the club unit economics that tell you which tier actually makes money.

Ecommerce & DTC Growth

MER is holding. Revenue is up. Profit is down. Every DTC founder in Santa Barbara County hits this point, usually between $5M and $20M, when ad costs, inventory working capital, and fulfillment complexity start eating the margin that used to be obvious. A fractional CFO builds the contribution margin model by SKU and channel, the inventory cash lock-up analysis, and the spend-to-cash bridge that tells you what is actually funding growth and what is just financing the next ad buy.

Health & Wellness Unit Economics

Longevity, IV therapy, hormone optimization, recovery, functional medicine. The Santa Barbara and Goleta wellness practice usually runs several modalities under one roof, each with its own acquisition cost, retention curve, and margin, all commingled in one P&L. A fractional CFO separates the lines, tells you which modality actually carries the practice and which one is quietly subsidized, and builds the membership and package economics that hold up as you add chairs and locations.

Elective Medicine & the Consult Lever

Cosmetic surgery, dermatology, MedSpa, cosmetic dental. In elective medicine the whole business turns on the consultation-to-procedure conversion and the mix of procedures you book. Most Santa Barbara County practices track production but never model the lever. A fractional CFO builds the per-procedure contribution math, the provider and room utilization read, and the marketing payback by service line so you grow the procedures that pay and stop subsidizing the ones that do not.
