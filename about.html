About Scott Hess

I Don't Report on the Business.
I Run Inside It.

Founder and Fractional CFO with 20+ years building finance functions that operationalize business decisions. Now focused on owner and founder-led companies under $50MM on the California coast.

Building Finance Functions That Drive the Business Forward

Most fractional CFOs hand you a report and leave. I embed inside your company and build the finance infrastructure that lets you actually run the business in real time.

I specialize in building finance functions that operationalize business decisions -- not just track them after the fact. My career spans subscription ecommerce, retail, and institutional finance. I've seen what works and what breaks at every stage of growth.

Today that experience is focused on a specific kind of company: owner and founder-led businesses under $50MM on the California coast, in three verticals I know well -- wineries, health and wellness, and elective medicine. These are operators who have outgrown their current finance setup. The close takes too long, the data is unreliable, and the owner is making decisions without real numbers. I fix that.

Before building Main Street IQ, I spent 12 years at Northern Trust in institutional finance -- modeling, M&A due diligence, and ERP implementation. That institutional rigor is what I bring to owner-operated companies that need it most.

I'm a Navy veteran with an MBA from Pepperdine's Graziadio School of Business. I bring discipline, structure, and operational intensity to every engagement.
