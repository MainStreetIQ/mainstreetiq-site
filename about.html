About Scott Hess

You Don't Want a CFO.
You Want to Know the Next Right Move.

That's what I build for owner and founder-led companies under $50MM: the numbers, the read, and the judgment, at whatever depth the decision needs. Founder and Fractional CFO, twenty years in finance and operations, building finance functions that turn numbers into decisions.

Numbers Are Only Worth What They Change

A report that tells you what's wrong and walks away is worse than no report. Everything I produce, from a market read to running your whole finance function, ends the same way: with a decision you can act on and a path forward.

How close I get depends on what the decision needs. For many owners the right help is intelligence and judgment that sharpen a call they are already making: a read on where buyers are going, what they cost to win, where the leaks are. I bring the analysis and the experience; you stay in the driver's seat. That work is built to be the front door to everything else.

When the stakes are higher, I get closer. Engage puts a CFO in your room while your team keeps running the books. Manage is your finance department, run by us: close, cash, dashboards, the whole machine, so you can go run the business. Same judgment, more involvement.

My career spans subscription ecommerce, retail, and institutional finance, so I have seen what works and what breaks at every stage of growth. Today that experience is focused on owner and founder-led businesses under $50MM, in four verticals I know well: DTC ecommerce, wineries, health and wellness, and elective medicine. We are rooted on the California coast and work with owner-operators nationwide. These are operators who have outgrown their current finance setup. The close takes too long, the data is unreliable, and the owner is making decisions without real numbers. I fix that.

Before Main Street IQ, I was at Northern Trust in institutional finance: modeling, M&A due diligence, and ERP implementation. I served as a Navy officer first. Main Street IQ is veteran-owned and operated.

How we work together

Two Ways I Work With Owners

I augment your leadership

A report, an audit, an ongoing read on your market and your numbers. You make the calls; I make sure you are making them with corporate-grade information instead of a gut feel. This is the Discover, Diagnose, and Monitor work.

I advise alongside your team

When you want a CFO in the decisions, not just in your inbox. Engage puts me in the room beside the finance staff you already have. Manage is your finance department, run by us. This is the Advise work.

You do not have to know which one you need on day one. Start with a single piece of intelligence and let the relationship find its level.
