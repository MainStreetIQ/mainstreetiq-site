The read and the call, for a San Luis Obispo business

An AI CFO pairs a named finance operator with a built-in intelligence engine. The engine does the grind, margin, cash, forecasting, reporting, that used to need a team. The operator, based here on the Central Coast, owns the decision. Here is where it lands hardest across San Luis Obispo County.

Across Paso Robles, Templeton, and the Edna Valley, the engine models barrel inventory, club economics, and the multi-year cash conversion cycle continuously; the operator names the tier that is losing money and builds the forecast that gets you to harvest.

Ecommerce & Growing Companies

For growing SLO County DTC and commercial operators, the engine reads margin by SKU and channel in real time. The AI CFO turns it into the pricing, inventory, and growth decisions that actually pencil out.
