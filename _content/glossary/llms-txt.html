llms.txt

llms.txt is a plain-text file placed at the root of a website that gives AI systems a clean, curated summary of what the site is about and which pages matter most, the way robots.txt speaks to search crawlers.

In Plain Language

What it means

As AI systems increasingly read websites to answer questions, site owners needed a way to hand those systems a clear, machine-friendly map instead of making them guess from cluttered HTML. llms.txt is an emerging convention that does exactly that: a Markdown file at yoursite.com/llms.txt summarizing the site and pointing to its key content.

It is not a ranking trick. It is a clarity tool: a clean summary that can help a machine that reads it get your site's facts right. Nobody has yet shown that it gets a business named in AI answers.

Adoption is early and support varies by AI system, and some systems do not read it at all. The cost of adding one is small, so it is reasonable housekeeping, but it is not where being recommended comes from.

Why it matters

Why it matters for lower middle market companies

At best, a clean llms.txt gives an assistant that reads it an accurate summary of your business. The signals the evidence so far ties to being named in AI answers sit off your site: reviews, and the third-party lists and articles that mention you.

It is a small, do-it-this-week task. The slower work of earning reviews and third-party mentions is what moves the answer. Main Street IQ maintains one for this site.
