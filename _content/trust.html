Platform + Practice

What We Use, and What We Build Ourselves

Manage runs on commercial AI tools used under business terms. The practice and security layers on top are built by Main Street IQ. Honest about which is which.

What we use

Commercial AI tools, configured for your engagement:

  • An AI model service. The inference layer for every skill output, used under business terms. Zero data retention is available from the provider as a per-organization arrangement; it is not yet in place for Main Street IQ.
  • Collaborative drafting and spreadsheet tools. Where your team and ours draft close narratives, board decks, and lender packages, and work through forecasts and scenario models together. Our practice is to keep high-sensitivity work out of these tools.
  • Network-isolated deployment (enterprise add-on). Available for clients who require AI processing inside their own cloud environment. Not the default; scoped per engagement.

What we build ourselves

The practice and security layers on top:

  • Credential broker. A local secrets store built by Main Street IQ, backed by the operating system keychain, that mints short-lived, narrowly scoped tokens one call at a time. Version one is built and tested against mock providers; live provider connections come next. Our rule in the meantime: no credential reaches a model in any form.
  • De-identification before AI. Our handling is designed so models work from aggregates and de-identified data rather than raw customer records. Customer identifiers are replaced with keys and leak-checked, and a guard blocks AI tools from reading raw client exports.
  • Per-client read scoping and structured audit log. A scoping layer stops a run for one client from reading another client's folder. Month-end close, P&L, P&L forecast and finance package runs append a structured audit entry to that client's folder, available to you on request.
  • Market-segment skills. Marketplace settlement reconciliation, cohort economics with long-window variants, marketing halo attribution, multi-location inventory, 3PL invoice forensics, cash-forecast drift detection.
  • Four market-segment packs. Ecommerce, wine, health and wellness, elective medicine. Different stacks, different cohort definitions, different compliance calendars.
  • Per-client CEO dashboard. Four tiers, phone-first, with the disclosure tier discipline (real / benchmark / fixture) we ship on every artifact.
  • The pre-publish gate. Every artifact that leaves the engagement is checked for canonical facts, scope, voice, factual accuracy, legal and regulatory risk, and the numbers behind it. Plus Scott signs every monthly close.
The Six Controls

Three From Our Providers. Three From Us.

Every embedded finance engagement should be able to name its security controls. Here are ours, and where each one stands today.

Zero Data Retention: Where It Stands

Our AI provider offers zero data retention as a per-organization arrangement, under which prompts and outputs are not stored after the response returns. It is not yet in place for Main Street IQ. If your engagement requires it, ask us where it stands.

No Training on Your Data

Under our AI provider's business terms, your data is not used to train its models. That holds whether or not zero data retention is in place.

Optional Network Isolation

For enterprise clients that require it, we can run AI processing inside your own cloud environment over a private connection, designed so financial data does not cross the public internet. Scoped per engagement, not the default. Architecture details on request.

Main Street IQ Credential Broker

A local secrets store built by Main Street IQ, backed by the operating system keychain, holds long-lived provider credentials and mints short-lived, narrowly scoped tokens one call at a time. Version one is built and tested against mock providers; live connections to QuickBooks and other providers come next. Our rule in the meantime: no credential reaches a model in any form.

De-identification + Client Scope

Our handling is designed so models see aggregates or de-identified data rather than raw rows: customer identifiers are replaced with keys and leak-checked, and a guard blocks AI tools from reading raw client exports. A scoping layer stops a run for one client from reading another client's folder.

Audit Log + Pre-Publish Gate

Month-end close, P&L, P&L forecast and finance package runs append a structured audit entry to that client's folder, available to you on request. Every artifact bound for outside our engagement (CPA, lender, board) passes through an integrity gate that checks facts, scope, voice, legal and regulatory risk, and the numbers.

Regulated Engagements

HIPAA Requirements and Data Residency

HIPAA. For health and wellness or elective medicine clients handling protected health information, an engagement requires a signed Business Associate Agreement and a HIPAA-enabled setup with the provider that runs the work. Ask us where that stands before sharing any PHI.

Data residency. Where your data is processed depends on how your engagement is set up. Ask us and we will tell you specifically.

Subprocessor list. Our full subprocessor list is maintained internally and kept current. Write to security@mainstreetiq.com and we will send it; how we share information is set out in our Privacy Policy.
