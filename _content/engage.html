Who This Is For

The Questions Already on Your Desk

Engage is built around the decisions you are already turning over. If these sound familiar, this is the shape that fits.

Can we afford the next hire, and when?

Your finance lead can tell you what payroll costs today. The harder question is what the hire does to cash and margin six months out, and that is the conversation we bring to the monthly session.

Will this price change hold?

A pricing move touches margin, volume, and the customers you most want to keep. We work it through with you before it goes live, not after the first month of results.

Which channels and product lines are actually making money?

Revenue by line is easy to report. Contribution by line, after the costs that belong to it, is where the decision lives.

Do we fund this from cash, or borrow for it?

Equipment, inventory, a new location. We put the cash picture next to the choice, so the decision is made on the numbers you will be living with.

What should next year's plan commit to?

Annual planning that starts from a measured read of where the business stands, so the plan is one your team can run against.

Engage and Manage

Two Shapes of the Same Relationship

Engage and Manage are the two ways into our Fractional CFO work. Engage is this page; Manage has its own. Here is the pair side by side.

Manage

We run the finance function outright. The close, the cash, the books, the reporting, and the dashboard as recurring work, with the CFO embedded weekly. Everything in Engage sits inside it.

The line between them: who owns the books. Engage advises while your team executes; Manage takes the function.
