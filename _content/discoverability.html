Why this matters now

Discovery moved, and most businesses did not

A prospect used to find you through a ranked page of links and a map of pins. That funnel is still there, but a new one now sits in front of it: the buyer asks an AI what to buy and who to trust, reads one synthesized answer, and often stops there.

That answer is not built from your website alone. The model assembles it from your reviews, your directory listings, the articles that mention you, the schema on your pages, and the wider citation footprint around your name. If those signals are thin or stale, you can be left out of the answer no matter how good your product is.

What founders ask about AI discoverability

AI discoverability is whether your business shows up when a prospect asks an AI tool who to buy from. When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for the best option in your category and region, the model names a short list. AI discoverability is whether your business is on that list, and why: the answer is built from classical search, reviews, listings and your wider citation footprint, not your website alone.

SEO optimizes for a ranked page of blue links a person scrolls. AI discoverability optimizes for a single synthesized answer a model hands back, where there is no page two. The signals overlap but they are not the same: the model builds its answer from your external citation footprint, not just your own pages, so the lever that moves AI visibility usually lives off your website.
