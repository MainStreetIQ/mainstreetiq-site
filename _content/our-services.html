The Practice

What a Fractional CFO Actually Does

And what the intelligence engine has to do with it.

Main Street IQ is a fractional CFO practice. A senior finance leader, embedded in your business, accountable for the close, the cash, the margins, the reporting, the board conversation, the next decision. The kind of judgment a $50MM company eventually needs full-time, priced and paced for the company you have today.

What makes it different is the instrument set the CFO arrives with. A fractional CFO can only read the business with the data already in it. We bring an intelligence engine that does the reading: market signals, cohort patterns, AI visibility, competitive position, the gaps in the buyer journey. The engine surfaces what is actually happening; the CFO works alongside you to decide what to do about it.

Main Street IQ provides fractional CFO and advisory services. We are not a CPA firm and do not provide audit, attest, or tax-preparation services.

Where You Sit With Us

Three Places You Can Be

Same practice, same instrument set. The right place depends on what you already have in-house: whether you only need the reading, you keep your finance team and want a CFO in the room, or you want us to be the finance department.

01

AI-Offering Client

In our data, not our room

Cadence: Monthly (Monitor), one-time (Audits, FBD) Best for: Teams with capable execution who need better signal

02

Engage

A CFO alongside your team

You keep your finance team. We bring the CFO and the intelligence engine to the decisions that actually move the business: pricing, hiring, capital, channel shifts, board prep. The engine briefs the room before each session, so the meeting starts at "here is what we are deciding" instead of "here is what is happening." For owner-operators who have execution covered and want senior judgment at the decision moments, not a CFO in the seat.

Who owns the books: You do. Your controller or finance lead runs daily execution. Best for: Owner-operators with finance staff in place

What's Included

A standing monthly working session with the operator and finance lead. Monitor Plus running underneath. Decision support on the named moments between sessions: pricing reviews, hiring decisions, capital choices, board prep, growth planning. The engine is the briefing artifact; the CFO is the judgment. How we build a strong finance operation: the RAID principles →

Where it stops: we do not run your books. Your controller or finance lead keeps daily execution; we bring senior judgment at the decisions, not the bookkeeping. Capital raises, sale processes, lender packages, and succession planning sit outside scope.

03

Manage

Your finance department, run by us

We are your finance function. We run the books: daily bookkeeping, monthly close, cash, cohorts, sales-tax and 1099 compliance prep, the whole stack, with the engine underneath and a named operator on your account. The CFO does the work a full-time CFO would do at a materially larger company. For owner-operators who would rather not build a finance team at all.

Who owns the books: We do. We are the team. Best for: Operators who want one accountable team running the whole function

What's Included

Embedded finance leadership covering close, cash, margin, investor and board reporting, KPI dashboards, cross-functional strategy, and finance team development. Monitor Plus running underneath. Capped at three active clients at any time, because the CFO time it takes is real. We build the function on the RAID principles → from the first close. See the full Manage page →

Where it stops: we are not your CPA or tax preparer, and we do not run marketing or operations. The named bookkeeper and your software stack are billed separately.
