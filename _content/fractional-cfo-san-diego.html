The Challenge

Why San Diego Companies Outgrow Their Finance Function

San Diego County has one of the strongest consumer-brand and wellness economies on the West Coast, and most of it is built by owner-operators. The same growth that makes these businesses exciting creates financial complexity that spreadsheets and a part-time bookkeeper cannot handle. These are the segments we know deepest.

DTC & Ecommerce Growth

MER is holding. Revenue is up. Profit is down. San Diego's DTC and consumer-brand scene, from coastal North County active-lifestyle brands to the operators working out of Downtown and North Park, hits this point somewhere on the way to eight figures: ad costs, inventory working capital, and fulfillment complexity start eating the margin that used to be obvious. A fractional CFO builds the contribution margin model by SKU and channel, the inventory cash lock-up analysis, and the spend-to-cash bridge that tells you what is actually funding growth and what is just financing the next ad buy.

Health & Wellness Unit Economics

Longevity, IV therapy, hormone optimization, recovery, functional medicine. San Diego's wellness market runs unusually deep, and the practice in La Jolla, Del Mar, or Encinitas usually runs several modalities under one roof, each with its own acquisition cost, retention curve, and margin, all commingled in one P&L. A fractional CFO separates the lines, tells you which modality actually carries the practice and which one is quietly subsidized, and builds the membership and package economics that hold up as you add chairs and locations.

Elective Medicine & the Consult Lever

Cosmetic surgery, cosmetic dermatology, MedSpa, cosmetic dental. The La Jolla and Del Mar corridors carry one of Southern California's densest concentrations of elective medicine practices, and in this business everything turns on the consultation-to-procedure conversion and the mix of procedures you book. Most practices track production but never model the lever. A fractional CFO builds the per-procedure contribution math, the provider and room utilization read, and the marketing payback by service line so you grow the procedures that pay and stop subsidizing the ones that do not.

Wine Country Economics, San Diego Scale

Ramona and the San Pasqual Valley anchor a real and growing wine scene, smaller than the Central Coast but built on the same punishing economics: multi-year production cycles, barrel inventory, club shipments, and tasting-room overhead all pulling cash in different directions. A fractional CFO builds the 13-week cash forecast that tells you in June whether you will make it to crush, and the per-tier club unit economics that tell you which tier actually makes money.

What the read looks like

Finding the SKUs that were financing the ad budget

Illustrative engagement

A DTC brand comes to us with top-line growth every quarter and less cash in the bank every month. The read: two hero SKUs carry the entire catalog, and the ad spend that looks efficient in the dashboard is buying revenue on the SKUs that lose money after freight and fulfillment.
