The Challenge

Why Owner-Operators Under $50M Are Stuck on AI

Every vendor is suddenly an AI vendor, every conference has an AI track, and the honest question, "where does this actually pay back in my business," goes unanswered. Here is what we see across Santa Barbara and San Luis Obispo counties.

The Noise Problem

You are being pitched AI chatbots, AI marketing, AI everything, mostly by people reselling the same tools with a markup. The pitches lead with the technology and never with your P&L. An owner-operator does not need an AI strategy deck. You need someone to look at your actual workflows, name the three places AI removes real cost or real risk, and tell you plainly what to skip.

Your Buyers Already Ask AI About You

When a prospect asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini who to buy from in your category, an answer comes back with names in it. Whether yours is one of them is now a revenue question, the same way page-one Google was fifteen years ago. Most owners have never once looked at what AI engines say about their business. We read that channel as part of the engagement, because it is already re-routing buyers.

The Payback Is in the Back Office

The unglamorous truth from running our own operation: the durable AI payback for a company under $50M is in the back office. Reconciliations, invoice checks, data pulls, report drafts, close prep, the repetitive rules-based work that eats staff hours every week. That is where we run AI agents in our own practice, and it is usually the first place we point a client, because the savings are measurable and the risk is controllable.

The Guardrail Problem

The fastest way to get burned is an AI workflow with no rules about data handling, accuracy, or review. We have watched pilots die because nobody decided what data was allowed in, and worse, workflows ship with nobody checking the output. Our position is simple: AI does the grind, humans own every judgment call, and anything that cannot be run safely does not get run. We publish how we hold ourselves to that on our own AI Use page.
