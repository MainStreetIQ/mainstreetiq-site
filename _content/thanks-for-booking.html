What to Expect

How the Call Runs

The call is structured but informal. Here's the rough shape:

  • First 5 minutes: Your context, company stage, what's working, what's broken in the finance function.
  • Next 15 minutes: The specifics, close cycle, margin visibility, cash forecasting, tech stack, what your current provider is doing (and not doing).
  • Last 10 minutes: Honest assessment, whether you need a fractional CFO at all, whether Main Street IQ is the right fit, and what I'd prioritize in the first 90 days if we worked together.

You'll leave with a recommendation. If we're not the right fit, I'll say so, and probably point you toward who is.
