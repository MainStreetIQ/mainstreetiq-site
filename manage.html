Who This Is For

If This Sounds Like You, Keep Reading

Manage exists because the typical fractional CFO + offshore bookkeeping + standalone analyst combination is failing for founders and CEOs at this scale. These are the specific failure modes we replace.

Your Monthly Close Has Errors You Are Catching, Not Them

Six of the last eight months had a P&L error your ops team noticed before the finance team did. Revenue booked into the wrong month, vendor bills that do not match what was agreed, payouts nobody reconciled. The cost is not just the dollars. It is the trust.

You Found Out About a Cash Gap by Living Through It

You took on a high-APR working capital line because the forecast did not see the gap coming. You would rather have a 13-week forecast, kept current on a cadence that fits your business, that alerts you when the low point dips under your cash floor, not a quarterly board slide reviewing what already happened.

You Cannot See Profitability by SKU, Channel, or Cohort

If you sell online, you have 50 SKUs and launch 50 more a year. You sell across Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop, wholesale, and direct. You run ads on six platforms. You have no idea which SKUs are dilutive, which channels are unprofitable, or what your cohort LTV-to-CAC really looks like at 30, 90, 180, and 365 days. Decisions are gut.

You Have Three Finance Vendors and No One Owns the Outcome

One firm does the books. Another does the CFO work. A third runs the dashboards or the analytics. When something is wrong, each one points at the others. You wanted accountability. You got coordination tax.

Your Current Setup Cannot Get You to 10 Percent Net

You are at 2 to 5 percent net margin. You want to get to 10. Your finance team can produce a P&L, but they cannot build the product and pricing plan, the vendor renegotiation case, the channel-mix reallocation, or the cost tightening that the climb requires. Results depend on your business, but you need a CFO who runs that plan, not just reports against it.

You Are Heading Into a Big Quarter and Cannot Afford a Surprise

One season carries a large share of your year. Your current setup got you through last year, but barely. You cannot enter peak season again with a finance function that is fragile, slow, and reactive.

What Manage Delivers

Four Workstreams. One Accountable Owner.

Manage works in four workstreams that map to how your business actually runs: automation does the procedural work, and your operator and Scott do the judgment and the signing. The outputs an in-house finance team would produce, on the cadence your business runs.

01 · Close & Reporting

Daily transaction categorization with human sign-off. Multi-channel marketplace settlement reconciliation across Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop, and your POS. 3PL and vendor invoice validation that flags overcharges weekly. FX revaluation for multi-currency operators. Monthly close signed by Scott on day eight, with accuracy tracked on every close. Variance commentary that explains what happened and why, not just what. The monthly CFO narrative your board, lender, or CPA can rely on.

02 · Cash & Working Capital

A 13-week cash forecast in a workbook you own, updated on a cadence set for your business from your bank balances, sales, QuickBooks bills and open invoices, payroll calendar, and committed spend, with what moved since the last update called out. Scenario planning when a decision calls for it, support on collections, and an alert when the low point dips below your cash floor.

03 · Unit Economics & Forecasting

SKU profitability matrix with named verdicts (hero, margin king, kill candidate). Channel and country P&L with marketplace-fee allocation per SKU. Cohort LTV and CAC at 30, 90, 180, and 365 days, plus 540 and 730 day variants for slow-reorder businesses. Marketing halo attribution for DTC ad spend that lifts marketplace sales but goes uncredited. Launch-cohort tracking for high-velocity SKU launches. Driver-based forecast you can edit live in Excel. Your per-client CEO dashboard. The growth team gets data that respects their existing CM dashboard; finance layers on what they cannot build.

04 · Compliance & Risk

Multi-state sales tax nexus monitoring across all your channels. Marketplace facilitator coverage verification (Amazon and TikTok collect-and-remit by state). 1099 prep and W9 collection. Audit-trail workpapers your CPA can file from. Estimated quarterly tax payment calculations. Segment-specific compliance (TTB for wine, HIPAA handling for medical under a signed Business Associate Agreement, sales tax + marketplace facilitator for ecom). Every report that leaves the engagement is checked before it ships.

The Cash Forecast

How the 13-Week Cash Forecast Works

One workbook, built for your business, kept current on a cadence that fits it, and read by us each time it is updated.

01

Built for Your Business

Your channels, your payroll, your cards

One Excel workbook that you own, shaped to how money actually moves through your company: the sales channels you run (online store, subscription, marketplaces, wholesale), your payroll cadence, each card's statement cycle, and the day your week starts. It opens from cash in the bank, not book cash.

02

Kept Current by Your Bookkeeper

On a cadence set for your business

Your bookkeeper enters the latest actuals and bank balances, pastes a sales report and the QuickBooks unpaid bills report (plus open invoices, if you sell on terms), and updates committed spend. How often depends on how fast cash moves in your business. The workbook turns sales into expected payouts on its own, and the routine is written into it.

03

Checked Before It Counts

A readiness check on every save

A built-in health check flags blank inputs and gaps before anyone relies on the numbers, and every finance package and cash flash states what that check reads.

04

Read Every Update

Low point, what moved, floor alert

We read each saved update: the lowest point in the next thirteen weeks, what moved since the last read, and an alert when that low point falls below the cash floor you set. It feeds the monthly finance package and a one-page cash and sales flash between packages.

How It Works

Who Does the Work, and Who Signs It

The model that lets a lower middle market company afford a full finance function. Automation does the procedural work; people do the judgment, the exceptions, the communication, and the signing. We build the whole function on the RAID principles: redundant, agile, intelligent, data-driven. Below is what each layer actually does.

01

Scott as Fractional CFO

The single accountable owner

Owns the relationship, signs every monthly close, runs the weekly office hours, drives the margin plan, leads vendor negotiations and the relationship with your bank, hires and manages the operator. Reviews and signs; does not produce.

02

Your Named Bookkeeper-Controller

US-based, dedicated to your account

Single senior operator who runs day-to-day execution: transaction sign-off, marketplace reconciliation, 3PL invoice validation, AR collections, AP discipline, sales tax filings, inventory accounting, monthly close production. Works directly with your logistics, supply chain, and growth teams. We source and contract the person; you get a named individual who knows your business.

03

On-Demand Analyst Pool

Scales with the work

For the analytical lifts that go beyond what the dashboard produces natively: bespoke scenario modeling, board and investor reporting packages, dashboard repairs when integrations break, ad-hoc analysis tied to the quarterly strategic review.

04

The Automation

Working in the background

Automation handles the procedural work: transaction categorization, marketplace reconciliation, cash forecast reads, customer cohort economics, vendor invoice flagging, dashboard refresh, compliance monitoring, and first-draft narrative. GAAP judgment, novel transactions, integration exceptions, vendor and 3PL negotiation, coordination with your team, and signing the close are human work. The automation speeds up the people; it does not replace judgment. A person reviews and signs every output.

05

Your Per-Client CEO Dashboard

Four views, one link, refresh as you need

Built fresh for your account on top of your data. A phone view for the 30-second morning glance. A desktop view for the weekly operating meeting. A close view for the month. A deep-dive view for the quarterly strategic review. Auth-gated to you and the people you designate.
