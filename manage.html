Who This Is For

If This Sounds Like You, Keep Reading

Manage exists because the typical fractional CFO + offshore bookkeeping + standalone analyst combination is failing for owner-operators at this scale. These are the specific failure modes we replace.

Your Monthly Close Has Errors You Are Catching, Not Them

Six of the last eight months had a P&L error your ops team noticed before the finance team did. 3PL overcharges, split shipments booked into the wrong month, marketplace fees not reconciled. The cost is not just the dollars. It is the trust.

You Found Out About a Cash Gap by Living Through It

You took on a high-APR working capital line because the forecast did not see the gap coming. You would rather a daily-refreshed 13-week forecast that alerts you the week before, not a quarterly board slide reviewing what already happened.

You Cannot See Profitability by SKU, Channel, or Cohort

You have 50 SKUs and launch 50 more a year. You sell across Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop, wholesale, and direct. You run ads on six platforms. You have no idea which SKUs are dilutive, which channels are unprofitable, or what your cohort LTV-to-CAC really looks like at 30, 90, 180, and 365 days. Decisions are gut.

You Have Three Finance Vendors and No One Owns the Outcome

One firm does the books. Another does the CFO work. A third runs the dashboards or the analytics. When something is wrong, each one points at the others. You wanted accountability. You got coordination tax.

Your Current Setup Cannot Get You to 10 Percent Net

You are at 2 to 5 percent net margin. You want to get to 10. Your finance team can produce a P&L, but they cannot build the SKU-rationalization plan, the 3PL renegotiation case, the channel-mix reallocation, or the marketing-efficiency tightening that the climb requires. Results depend on your business, but you need a CFO who runs that plan, not just reports against it.

You Are Heading Into a Big Quarter and Cannot Afford a Surprise

Q4 is 40 percent of your year. Your current setup got you through last year, but barely. You cannot enter peak season again with a finance function that is fragile, slow, and reactive.

How It Works

Three Humans, Five Agents, One Owner

The model that lets a sub-$50M company afford a finance function that used to require four to eight people. The compression is real, and so is the honesty: the agents do the procedural work; humans do the judgment, the exceptions, the communication, and the signing. We build the whole function on the RAID principles: redundant, agile, intelligent, data-driven. Below is what each layer actually does.

01

Scott as Fractional CFO

~10 hours per week per client, hard cap

Owns the relationship, signs every monthly close, runs the weekly office hours, drives the margin plan, owns vendor and financing negotiations, hires and manages the operator. Reviews and signs; does not produce. The single accountable owner of the function.

02

Your Named Bookkeeper-Controller

~80 hours per month, US-based, dedicated to your account

Single senior operator who runs day-to-day execution: transaction sign-off, marketplace reconciliation, 3PL invoice validation, AR collections, AP discipline, sales tax filings, inventory accounting, monthly close production. Works directly with your logistics, supply chain, and growth teams. We source and contract the person; you get a named individual who knows your business.

03

On-Demand Analyst Pool

~20 hours per month average, scales up for deep-dive work

For the analytical lifts that go beyond what the dashboard produces natively: bespoke scenario modeling, board-style packages for lenders or investors, dashboard repairs when integrations break, ad-hoc analysis tied to the quarterly strategic review.

04

Five AI Agents

Always on, in the background

Controller, Treasurer, FP&A, Tax & Compliance, and CFO agents running 49 specialized skills across daily categorization, marketplace reconciliation, cash forecasting, cohort economics, vendor invoice flagging, dashboard refresh, and compliance monitoring. They handle the roughly seventy to eighty percent of finance work that is procedural: data pulls, reconciliation, deterministic math, first-draft narrative, threshold alerts. The remaining twenty to thirty percent (GAAP judgment, novel-transaction categorization, integration exception handling, vendor and 3PL negotiation, client-team coordination, and signing the close) is human work. The agents accelerate the humans; they do not replace judgment. Every output a human reviews and a human signs.

05

Your Per-Client CEO Dashboard

Four tiers, one URL, refresh-as-you-need

Built fresh for your account on top of your data. Phone-first Tier 1 for the 30-second morning glance. Desktop Tier 2 for the weekly operating meeting. Tier 3 for the monthly close. Tier 4 for the quarterly strategic deep dive. Auth-gated to you and the people you designate.
