Center of Influence

For the accountants, bankers, and advisors who already trust their clients to us.

How this works

Owner-operators between $5M and $50M in revenue rarely shop for a fractional CFO. They accept a warm introduction from someone they already trust. That is where you come in.

The Center of Influence program is a small, deliberate network of professionals who already know our work and introduce us to clients we can help. In exchange, you earn a commission on the engagements that follow: 15% one-time on diagnostic projects, 10% monthly for the first 12 months of any retainer. Paid 30 days after we collect on projects, monthly on retainers.

Who fits

  • CPAs and tax advisors with clients between $5M and $50M who keep outgrowing the books.
  • Commercial bankers and wealth advisors whose clients run real operating businesses on the California coast.
  • Insurance, payroll, and benefits brokers who sit close to owner decisions.
  • Other fractional executives (COO, CHRO) where the engagement scope does not overlap.
