Center of Influence

For the accountants, attorneys, bankers, and advisors who already trust their clients to us.

How this works

Owner-operators between $5M and $50M in revenue rarely shop for a fractional CFO. They accept a warm introduction from someone they already trust. That is where you come in.

The Center of Influence program is a small, deliberate network of professionals who already know our work and introduce us to clients we can help. In exchange, two things flow back to you:

  • A 50% off voucher on any of our diagnostic projects, which you can hand to anyone in your network who would benefit.
  • A commission on the engagements that follow (15% one-time on projects, 10% monthly for the first 12 months of any retainer), where your firm's rules allow it.

For attorneys whose bar rules restrict referral fees, the relationship runs on mutual professional courtesy: the voucher is yours to give, the commission is waived, and the reciprocity is the value.

What to expect on the call

Twenty minutes. We talk through who you serve, where you see your clients get stuck, and whether the kind of work I do would be useful to them. No pitch, no slide deck. If we are not a fit for your network, I will tell you and suggest where to point clients instead.

If we have not spoken yet, use the calendar to find a time that works. If you already have the one-pager from me, treat this as the place to book the intro call.
