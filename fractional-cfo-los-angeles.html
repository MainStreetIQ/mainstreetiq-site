The Challenge

Why LA Owner-Operators Hire a Fractional CFO

Los Angeles is the country's densest DTC ecommerce cluster and one of its deepest markets for wellness and elective medicine. It is also a market where owner-operated brands compete every day against venture-funded rivals who can afford to lose money longer than you can. That fight is won on economics, and these are the four verticals where we know the economics deepest.

Ecommerce & DTC Contribution Math

MER is holding. Revenue is up. Profit is down. Every DTC founder hits this point, usually between $5M and $20M, when ad costs, inventory working capital, and fulfillment complexity start eating the margin that used to be obvious. In LA the squeeze is sharper because the brand next door is venture-funded and pricing for growth, not profit. A fractional CFO builds the contribution margin model by SKU and channel, the inventory cash lock-up analysis, and the spend-to-cash bridge that tells you what is actually funding growth and what is just financing the next ad buy.

Wine & DTC Club Economics

Los Angeles is not wine country, but wine business runs through it: urban wineries and tasting rooms inside the county, and wine brands whose club members and DTC buyers are concentrated here. Club tiers, shipping and compliance costs, and tasting-room overhead each carry their own margin story, and most P&Ls blur them together. A fractional CFO builds the per-tier club unit economics and the cash conversion picture that tell you which tier actually makes money and which one is quietly subsidized.

Health & Wellness Unit Economics

Longevity, IV therapy, hormone optimization, recovery, functional medicine. From Santa Monica down through Manhattan Beach and the South Bay, the LA wellness practice usually runs several modalities under one roof, each with its own acquisition cost, retention curve, and margin, all commingled in one P&L. A fractional CFO separates the lines, tells you which modality actually carries the practice and which one is quietly subsidized, and builds the membership and package economics that hold up as you add chairs and locations.

Elective Medicine & the Consult Lever

Cosmetic surgery, dermatology, MedSpa, injectables, cosmetic dental. The Beverly Hills and West Hollywood corridor is one of the most competitive elective medicine markets anywhere, and the whole business turns on the consultation-to-procedure conversion and the mix of procedures you book. Most practices track production but never model the lever. A fractional CFO builds the per-procedure contribution math, the provider and room utilization read, and the marketing payback by service line so you grow the procedures that pay and stop subsidizing the ones that do not.

CFO Services Across Los Angeles County

Main Street IQ is based on California's Central Coast, and Los Angeles County is one of the six coastal counties we serve. The work runs remotely by default, with on-site presence for the moments that deserve it. These are the corridors where our four verticals concentrate.

Santa Monica, Venice & the Westside

The Westside carries one of the heaviest concentrations of DTC and consumer-product brands in the country, owner-operated companies selling through their own site, Amazon, wholesale, and retail at the same time. For these brands we build the contribution margin read by SKU and channel, the inventory working-capital plan, and the ad-spend payback math that keeps growth funded by the business rather than by the next round someone else raised.

Beverly Hills & West Hollywood

The county's signature elective medicine corridor: cosmetic surgery, cosmetic dermatology, MedSpa, injectables, cosmetic dentistry, and adult cosmetic orthodontics. Competition here is intense and marketing costs reflect it. We build the per-procedure contribution model, the consult-to-procedure conversion read, and the marketing payback by service line that separate the practices compounding from the practices churning.

Manhattan Beach & the South Bay

The South Bay pairs a dense wellness economy, longevity, recovery, IV therapy, functional medicine, with ecommerce operators who live close to the port and their own inventory. For wellness practices we separate modality-level margins and build membership economics that scale; for the DTC operators we model inventory cash lock-up and fulfillment cost per order before those numbers quietly decide the year.

Pasadena, the Valley & Greater LA

Owner-operated businesses run deep across Pasadena, Burbank, Glendale, Long Beach, and the San Fernando Valley: family-owned brands, practices, and urban wineries and tasting rooms among them. What the read looks like

Naming the SKU that was quietly losing money

Illustrative engagement

A DTC brand comes to us with revenue climbing and cash tightening every quarter. The blended ROAS looks fine, so everyone keeps spending. The read: one hero SKU is carrying the whole catalog, and the ad budget is spread across products that lose money on every order after freight, fees, and returns. We build the contribution margin model by SKU and channel, reallocate spend to the products that actually pay, and the cash stops leaking without revenue giving anything back.
