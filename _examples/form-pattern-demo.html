MSIQ Form Pattern Demo

All form patterns used across the site, in one place. Use as a reference when building new forms. Loads /assets/css/forms.css and /assets/js/forms.js.

1. Single-step form (lead capture)

Use for: contact form, simple lead capture, WCIR signup, Fit Call free booking confirmation. Native form submit (no JS controller needed unless you want custom validation hooks).

Tell us about your business

We'll respond within one business day with a written one-pager.

Optional. The more you share, the sharper our one-pager.

Got it. We'll be in touch.

Look for an email from scott@mainstreetiq.com within one business day.

2. Multi-step form (assessment quiz)

Use for: Winery Assessment, Wellness Assessment, Elective Assessment, Strategic Brief intake. Progressive disclosure, per-step validation, choice-card UX, conditional branching support.

Which best describes your business?

3. Inline form (newsletter / hero CTA)

Use for: footer newsletter, hero email capture, any single-input form where you want a horizontal layout.

The current footer-newsletter on every page can be migrated to this pattern. Same fields, same backend, cleaner styling.
