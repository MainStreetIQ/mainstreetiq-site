Who AI recommends

45 of 442 wineries account for 47.9% of all AI namings

When a visitor asks an AI assistant where to taste, the assistant writes the shortlist. In our latest measurement, AI assistants gave us 2,644 answers on where to taste across the two counties and named a winery 23,232 times.

The 45 wineries named most often, the top tenth of the 442 we measure, account for 47.9% of those namings. The ten named most often account for 17.6%. 56 of the 442 were never named in an answer to their own county's questions.

And travelers are asking. MMGY Travel Intelligence's summer 2026 survey of 4,371 U.S. adults found that 52% of active leisure travelers have used AI to help plan a trip, up 10 points in a year.

Our read: the shortlist is increasingly written by AI, and a small group of wineries is on it most of the time.

What changed this quarter

Since Q2, 40 wineries were named less often and 15 more often

We asked the same 24 questions as last quarter, three times each. Of the 393 wineries we measured both times (we measure 442 this quarter; the difference is wineries that joined our census since then), 40 came up less often and 15 more often. In Santa Barbara County 22 came up less often and 8 more often; in San Luis Obispo County, 18 and 7. About as many wineries were named overall; what changed is which ones.

Most of the drop came from Perplexity and Gemini, the two of the four assistants we asked these questions that search the web for every answer. Across the wineries that fell, the times they were named went from 343 to 225, and of those 118 fewer namings, 99 came from those two.

Our read is that the change is in what those searches now find, not necessarily at the wineries. A fall here is a change in the answers, measured; it is not a finding about the winery. Ask an AI assistant where to taste in your county and see who appears. The full report names the largest moves each way.

Digital readiness

Five things most wineries get wrong

We also score every winery out of 100 on seven categories, for how easy it is to find, trust, book and buy from online. JUSTIN Vineyards & Winery leads the two counties at 81.7. These are the five gaps we found most often across the 442, each counted only where we could check.

Not in the search results for "wine tasting" searches 338 of 442
No press on the first page of its own name search 332 of 442
No tasting notes on the site 309 of 438 checked
Not in the Google Maps results for discovery searches 273 of 442
No tasting fee on a tasting page 246 of 418 checked

Our read: each gap is a question a visitor, or an assistant answering for one, cannot get answered from you. A visitor who searches for wineries rather than for you by name never sees you, and an assistant asked what a tasting costs has nothing from your own site to repeat.
