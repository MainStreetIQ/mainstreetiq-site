The Challenge

Why Orange County Companies Outgrow Their Finance Function

Orange County is one of the most competitive consumer markets in the country: high rents, high wages, sophisticated buyers, and a deep bench of competitors in every category. Owner-operated companies here do not get to coast on a good product; the margin math has to be right. These are the four verticals we know deepest.

Ecommerce & DTC Growth

The corridor from Irvine through Costa Mesa and Newport is one of the strongest consumer-brand and DTC ecommerce clusters in Southern California, and every founder in it hits the same wall: MER is holding, revenue is up, profit is down. Ad costs, inventory working capital, and fulfillment complexity quietly eat the margin that used to be obvious. A fractional CFO builds the contribution margin model by SKU and channel, the inventory cash lock-up analysis, and the spend-to-cash bridge that tells you what is actually funding growth and what is just financing the next ad buy.

Elective Medicine & the Consult Lever

Cosmetic surgery, dermatology, MedSpa, injectables, cosmetic dental. The Newport Beach corridor is one of the densest elective-medicine markets in the country, which means acquisition is expensive and the whole business turns on the consultation-to-procedure conversion and the mix of procedures you book. Most practices track production but never model the lever. A fractional CFO builds the per-procedure contribution math, the provider and room utilization read, and the marketing payback by service line so you grow the procedures that pay and stop subsidizing the ones that do not.

Health & Wellness Unit Economics

Longevity, IV therapy, hormone optimization, recovery, functional medicine. Orange County's wellness market is deep and demanding, and the typical practice runs several modalities under one roof, each with its own acquisition cost, retention curve, and margin, all commingled in one P&L, all paying Orange County rent. A fractional CFO separates the lines, tells you which modality actually carries the practice and which one is quietly subsidized, and builds the membership and package economics that hold up as you add chairs and locations.

Wine & DTC Club Economics

Our winery practice is anchored on the Central Coast, where we work inside wine country every week. For Orange County wine businesses, urban wineries, tasting rooms, and DTC wine brands selling into one of the strongest consumer markets in the state, the same discipline applies: club unit economics by tier, the cash conversion cycle across vintages and inventory, and the tasting-room and shipping math that tells you which part of the business actually makes money.

Irvine, Costa Mesa & the Consumer-Brand Cluster

The Irvine-to-Costa Mesa corridor holds one of the strongest concentrations of DTC ecommerce and consumer-product brands in Southern California. These are exactly the businesses where growth outruns the finance function: ad spend scales, inventory buys get bigger, channels multiply, and the P&L stops telling the truth. We build the SKU and channel contribution model, the cohort economics, and the cash forecast that put the founder back in control of the growth engine.

Newport Beach & the Elective Medicine Corridor

Newport Beach and its corridor make up one of the densest elective-medicine and wellness markets in the country: cosmetic surgery, dermatology, MedSpas, longevity clinics, and recovery studios competing for the same affluent patient. In a market this competitive, the practices that win are the ones that know their per-procedure contribution and their marketing payback by service line cold. That is the modeling we bring.

Anaheim, Santa Ana, Fullerton & North County

North Orange County runs on owner-operated companies: consumer products, light manufacturing, distribution, and the operating businesses that grew up alongside the region's visitor economy. These are founders who feel the weight of the next decision, a facility move, a product line, a key hire, and need CFO-level judgment without a full-time executive on payroll. We bring the margin analysis, the forecasting, and the reporting infrastructure that make those decisions clear.

Huntington Beach, Laguna & South County

From Huntington Beach down through Laguna, Mission Viejo, and San Clemente, the pattern is the lifestyle consumer brand and the boutique practice: surf and outdoor DTC, wellness studios, aesthetic practices, businesses built on a strong brand in a high-cost, high-competition market. We build the unit economics and cash visibility that keep the margin intact while the brand grows.
