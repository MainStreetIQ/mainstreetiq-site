In One Sentence

An AI CFO, Defined

An AI CFO is a fractional Chief Financial Officer whose work is powered by a built-in intelligence engine. It pairs an experienced human finance operator with AI-assisted analysis, forecasting, and reporting, so an owner-operated business gets the strategic read on its numbers and the decision that follows, faster and at a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire. The distinction that matters most: an AI CFO is a service delivered by a person, not a software product you log into.

The confusion is understandable. Half the finance-software market now calls itself an "AI CFO," because it sounds like the future and it sells subscriptions. But a tool that forecasts your cash or auto-categorizes your expenses is still a tool. It hands you an output and waits for you to decide what to do with it. That gap, between the output and the decision, is exactly where a CFO earns their keep.

An AI CFO closes that gap. The AI grinds through the analysis, the modeling, and the reporting that used to require a finance team. The operator brings the judgment: which cohort is worth chasing, which price move quietly adds margin, whether this growth push is fundable. You get both, in one engagement, without a full-time salary.

The Work

What an AI CFO Actually Does

Not "uses AI." Every provider says that now. Here's the work an AI CFO owns, and how the intelligence engine changes it.

Reads the Real Margin

Contribution margin by SKU, channel, and cohort, not a company-wide P&L. The engine crunches the data continuously; the operator tells you which products actually fund the business and which quietly drain it.

Forecasts the Cash

A 13-week rolling cash forecast that maps every timing gap: ad spend, inventory, AR, payroll. AI-assisted, always current, and read by someone who knows what to do when a gap opens up.

Prices with Evidence

Pricing decisions backed by unit economics and elasticity, not gut feel. The engine models the scenarios; the operator makes the call and owns the outcome.

Builds the Board and Lender Story

Data rooms, board decks, and lender-ready reporting with cohort analysis and capital efficiency baked in. The reporting an institution actually wants to see, produced in a fraction of the usual time.

Owns the Tech Stack

Modern AP/AR automation, close tools, and BI that updates in real time, wired together and actually used. The finance function rebuilt so the numbers are trustworthy and fast.

Answers "Is This Fundable?"

The question every owner-operator eventually asks. An AI CFO gives you the modeled answer and the judgment behind it, before you commit the capital, not in the post-mortem.

The Honest Distinction

"AI CFO Software" Is Not an AI CFO

There's a wave of well-funded software that markets itself as the "AI CFO," and some of it is genuinely good at what it does: automating the FP&A workflow, generating forecasts, answering questions about your own data in plain language. If you have a finance team to drive it, that software can be a real force multiplier.

But most owner-operated businesses under $50MM don't have that team. They have a bookkeeper, a CPA at tax time, and a founder making capital decisions on instinct. Handing that founder a more powerful dashboard doesn't fill the gap; it just gives them a faster way to look at a decision they still have to make alone.

An AI CFO fills the gap with a person. The intelligence engine does what the software does, and then a finance operator does the part the software can't: decide. That's the whole distinction, and for most Main Street businesses, it's the part that actually moves the number.
