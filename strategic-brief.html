Your Strategic Brief

This is the document every Main Street IQ engagement is built on. It takes 20 to 40 minutes. Answer in plain language, the way you'd explain your business to a friend. There are no wrong answers, only useful ones.

Step 1 of 7

Who you want walking in the door

Your ideal customer, in plain language.

Speak in plain language. Imagine telling a friend who that customer is.

Be specific where you can. We use this to filter session and traffic data.

First names or initials are fine. We use these as anchors, not for outreach.

What do they say, do, or buy that makes them unmistakable?

Who you do not want

The clearer this is, the better we filter.

Example: reviews mentioning "kid-friendly" would be a signal we lost the audience for a luxury brand.

What you are trying to achieve

Your objective, your targets, and the posture you run.

Up to three quantitative targets. Leave any row blank if you don't have it.

Pick the posture you actually run, not what sounds best. Volume isn't lesser than Premium; it's a different model.

Paint the picture. We use this to recognize success when it shows up.

How you measure success today

What you track, what you wish you tracked, and what would warn you.

What signals would warn you 60-90 days before revenue moved?

Example: Yelp review volume going up could mean mass-market drift for an exclusive brand.

Optional but recommended. We share a simple form once a month.

Channel and tactic priorities

Where to lean in, where to hold, where to pull back.

Brand non-negotiables

The lines we should not cross.

e.g. producer-led, serious, founder-direct.

Imagery style, color preferences, things to avoid.
