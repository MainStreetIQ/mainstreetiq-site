The Practice

What a Fractional CFO Actually Does

And what the intelligence engine has to do with it.

Main Street IQ is a fractional CFO practice. A senior finance leader, embedded in your business, accountable for the close, the cash, the margins, the reporting, the board conversation, the next decision. The kind of judgment a $50MM company eventually needs full-time, priced and paced for the company you have today.

What makes it different is the instrument set the CFO arrives with. Most fractional engagements ask the CFO to read your business using whatever data the operator already has, which is rarely enough. We bring an intelligence engine that does the reading: market signals, cohort patterns, AI visibility, competitive position, the gaps in the buyer journey. The engine surfaces what is actually happening; the CFO works alongside you to decide what to do about it.

Main Street IQ provides fractional CFO and advisory services. We are not a CPA firm and do not provide audit, attest, or tax-preparation services.

The Instrument Set

Four Readings, One Engine

The intelligence engine reads your business four ways; the CFO practice decides what to do about it. Every engagement uses the same instruments. The depth and cadence change with the shape of how we work together.

Discover

What is happening in your market right now. Cohort dynamics, regional shifts, the buyer behavior that is actually in motion. Surfaced as market intelligence reports and vertical scans.

Diagnose

What is happening in your business. The Website Audit and Buyer Alignment work that names exactly where the funnel leaks, where the messaging misses, and what an AI engine answers when a buyer asks about you. Surfaced as written diagnostics, scored against the same dimensions every time.

Monitor

What changes over time. The cadence that catches drift before it becomes a problem: AI visibility moves, competitive shifts, cohort behavior, the metrics that matter to the next decision. Surfaced as monthly scorecards.

Advise

What to do about it. The standing decision cadence where the CFO is in the decisions with you, reads the engine's findings against where the business is going, and works the next move with you. Surfaced as meeting cadence, decisions logged, follow-through tracked.

What's in the Instrument Set

The Offerings Behind Each Reading

Four productized verbs. Each one steps up our involvement on a different axis: Discover widens the lens, Diagnose deepens the synthesis, Monitor lengthens the relationship, Advise puts the CFO in the decisions with you.

01 | Discover

What is happening in the market

We read the market, you read the report

The lightest engagement we offer. We do not look at your business; we look at the world your business sells into. You take what we surface and decide what to do with it. Wine-only as a productized deliverable today; the same verb describes the first weeks of any Engage or Manage onboarding.

Wine Discover | Single Issue

One regional scan. Cohort dynamics, AI-visibility patterns across the region, buyer-behavior shifts. One-time purchase, branded PDF.

Wine Discover | Annual Subscription

All issues in the current year plus access to the back catalog. Quarterly drop cadence; auto-delivered, cancel anytime.

02 | Diagnose

What is happening in your business

A one-shot read, depth scales with the question

This is where our involvement steps up. We look at your funnel, your messaging, your AI surface, your competitive position. Four products at increasing scope, each a complete artifact in itself. Run any one of them standalone, or stack them.

Website Audit

Three-layer SEO / GEO / AEO read on a single URL. Where the funnel leaks, what AI engines say when buyers ask about you, where the messaging misses. Branded PDF with severity, evidence, recommendation, and expected outcome per finding.

Website Audit Pro

Adds the specialist interpretive layer on the operator's own surface: sequencing (which gate must be open before downstream fixes pay off), strategic positioning, operator-depth gotchas, vertical commentary on every finding. Where the base Audit is the punch list, Pro is the diagnosis. Includes the readout call by default.

Buyer Alignment Audit

Reverse-engineers the customer your public properties (web, social, ads, reviews, GBP, founder and team LinkedIn) imply you are attracting, then compares against the customer you say you want. Output is a three-mode failure classification (wrong person, right person wrong messaging, right person fragmented signal) plus unit-economics implications and a sequenced path forward.

Full Business Diagnostic (FBD)

Whole-business synthesis. Reads the Audit, the buyer-alignment work, and the monitor signals against your structured intake. Thirteen fully-formatted findings across seventeen dimensions of the business, opportunity sizing across five to seven levers, a 90-day roadmap, partner recommendations matched to specific findings. The deepest single artifact we sell outside a retainer. Available with a 90-minute strategy session.

Where these stop: a diagnostic names the work and quantifies the upside. It does not execute the fixes or stand watch over time. Execution stays with your team or your partners; the ongoing version of the read is Monitor.

03 | Monitor

What is changing over time

We keep watching, you get the scorecard

The recurring relationship. Same instruments as Diagnose, pointed at change over time rather than a single moment. This is where the practice becomes ongoing, and the structural bridge into the room.

Monitor

Monthly scorecard tracking your AI visibility, your competitive position, and the metrics tied to your next decision. Single-business view; the cadence catches drift before it becomes a problem.

Monitor Plus

Adds the cohort overlay. You against four named competitors, scored on the same dimensions you are scored on, every month. Plus specifically means cohort; additional named peers beyond the four are priced separately.

Where these stop: Monitor reads and reports; it does not put the CFO in the room or act on the findings. When the cadence keeps surfacing decisions you would rather not make alone, that is Engage.

04 | Advise

What to do about it

We are in the decisions with you

The deepest involvement we offer. Reading the business is upstream work; at some point somebody has to decide. This is where the CFO works those decisions alongside you, with the engine briefing the room before each one.

Engage

Senior CFO judgment on a standing cadence, with Monitor Plus running underneath. A monthly strategic-oversight session and a quarterly review with a refreshed roadmap. You keep your finance team and you own the books; we bring the judgment to pricing, hiring, capital, and channel decisions.

Manage

We run the finance function outright. The close, the cash, the books, the reporting, and the dashboard as recurring work, with the CFO embedded weekly. Everything in Engage sits inside it.

The line between them: who owns the books. Engage advises while your team executes; Manage takes the function. Both are scoped on an Intro Call rather than priced off a page, because the shape depends on what you already have in-house.

How It Stacks

Each Step Builds on the Last

Nothing here is a dead end. The work you buy at one level becomes the input to the next, so starting small is never starting over. Two ladders carry almost everything we do.

The one-time read, going deeper

Each rung keeps everything below it and adds a layer of synthesis.

Website Audit

The foundation. A three-layer read of one site: where the funnel leaks, what AI engines say about you, where the messaging misses.

Website Audit Pro

Adds the strategic read on top of the audit: what to fix first, the battles to skip, operator-depth detail, and the readout call.

Full Business Diagnostic

Adds the whole-business synthesis: reads the Audit Pro plus a Monitor run against your intake, sizes the opportunity, and builds the 90-day roadmap.

FBD Pro

Adds the per-line breakdown for businesses competing on several fronts, plus the Buyer Alignment work as the buyer-depth layer.

The ongoing relationship, going further

Each rung keeps everything below it and brings us closer to the room.

Monitor

The foundation. A monthly scorecard on your own surface, catching drift before it becomes a problem.

Monitor Plus

Adds the cohort overlay: you against four named competitors, scored on the same dimensions, every month.

Engage

Adds the CFO in the room: Monitor Plus runs underneath while we work the decisions with you. You keep your finance team and own the books.

Manage

Adds the whole finance function: we run the books, the close, the cash, and the reporting, with the CFO embedded weekly.

The same idea, stated plainly:

Website Audit Pro = Website Audit + the strategic read
Full Business Diagnostic = Website Audit Pro + a Monitor run + your intake
FBD Pro = Full Business Diagnostic + Buyer Alignment + the per-line breakdown
Monitor Plus = Monitor + your named-peer cohort
Engage = Monitor Plus + a CFO in the room
Manage = Engage + we run the books
