Fractional CFO

What a Fractional CFO Actually Does

And what the intelligence engine has to do with it.

Fractional CFO is one of Main Street IQ's two lines of work; the other, Wine Country Intelligence, is AI advisory for wineries. A fractional CFO is a senior finance leader, embedded in your business, accountable for the close, the cash, the margins, the reporting, the board conversation, the next decision. The kind of judgment a larger company eventually needs full-time, priced and paced for the company you have today.

What makes it different is the instrument set the CFO arrives with. A fractional CFO can only read the business with the data already in it. We bring an intelligence engine that does the reading: market signals, cohort patterns, AI visibility, competitive position, the gaps in the buyer journey. The engine surfaces what is actually happening; the CFO works alongside you to decide what to do about it. AI search visibility and AI consulting are part of how this work runs, not separate services.

Main Street IQ provides fractional CFO and advisory services. We are not a CPA firm and do not provide audit, attest, or tax-preparation services.

Where You Sit With Us

Three Places You Can Be

The right place depends on what you already have in-house: whether you only need the written findings, you keep your finance team and want a CFO in the room, or you want us to be the finance department.

01

Reports and Audits

Written findings, no CFO in the room

You get the written findings and your team takes the actions. We are accountable for the findings. You are accountable for what happens next. Every report is reviewed and signed by the CFO before it reaches you. How we use AI →

Cadence: One-time (Website Audit); quarterly or monthly for wineries Best for: Teams with capable execution who need better signal

What's Included

For any business: the Website Audit or Website Audit Pro, a written read of where your site and your AI search presence lose buyers. For wineries: the Wine Country Intelligence Report county edition, and Monitor or Monitor Plus to track your winery month over month. Each one stands alone, and the prices are on each page.

Where it stops: the CFO is not in the room. The findings land with your team, and your team decides what to do with them.

02

Engage

A CFO alongside your team

You keep your finance team. We bring the CFO and the intelligence engine to the decisions that actually move the business: pricing, hiring, capital allocation, channel shifts, annual planning. The engine briefs the room before each session, so the meeting starts at "here is what we are deciding" instead of "here is what is happening." For founders and CEOs who have execution covered and want senior judgment at the decision moments, not a CFO in the seat.

Who owns the books: You do. Your controller or finance lead runs daily execution. Best for: Founders and CEOs with finance staff in place

What's Included

A standing monthly working session with you and your finance lead. The intelligence engine running underneath. Decision support on the named moments between sessions: pricing reviews, hiring decisions, capital choices, annual and growth planning. The engine is the briefing artifact; the CFO is the judgment. How we build a strong finance operation: the RAID principles →

Where it stops: we do not run your books. Your controller or finance lead keeps daily execution; we bring senior judgment at the decisions, not the bookkeeping. Capital raises, sale processes, lender packages, and succession planning sit outside scope; we work alongside your bankers and advisors and refer that work out.

03

Manage

Your finance department, run by us

We are your finance function. We run the books: daily bookkeeping, monthly close, cash, customer cohorts, sales-tax and 1099 compliance prep, the whole stack, with the engine underneath and a named operator on your account. The CFO does the work a full-time CFO would do at a materially larger company. For founders and CEOs who would rather not build a finance team at all.

Who owns the books: We do. We are the team. Best for: Operators who want one accountable team running the whole function

What's Included

Embedded finance leadership covering close, cash, margin, investor and board reporting, KPI dashboards, cross-functional strategy, and finance team development. The intelligence engine running underneath. Limited to three clients at a time. We build the function on the RAID principles → from the first close. See the full Manage page →

Where it stops: we are not your CPA or tax preparer, and we do not run marketing or operations. The named bookkeeper and your software stack are billed separately. We run finance; we do not replace your accountant or your operators. Capital raises, sale processes, lender packages, and succession planning sit outside scope; we work alongside your bankers and advisors and refer that work out.

Moving between them. Some clients start with a report or an audit and move to Engage when they want a CFO in the decisions; others stay where they started. Either is a good place to be.
