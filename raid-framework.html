How We Operate

The RAID Framework

A finance function should not depend on any one person, run on manual work, or hand you the past instead of the next move. RAID is how we build and run one that does the opposite. It is the operating standard behind every Engage and Manage engagement.

The Idea

Four Principles, One Finance Function

Most finance teams grow by adding people to broken processes. RAID grows the function by fixing the process first, then letting systems and judgment do the scaling. Four principles turn finance from a bottleneck into a competitive advantage.

R

Redundant

Documented, cross-trained processes. No single points of failure. The finance function runs whether any one person is in the building or not.

A

Agile

Automation-first. We build systems before we add headcount. Technology handles the repetitive work so the people handle the thinking.

I

Intelligent

The finance team operates as owner-operators, not scorekeepers. They do not just report what happened. They drive what happens next.

D

Data-Driven

Every owner of a workstream carries three to five KPIs tied to real business levers. Real-time visibility into cash, margin, and the metrics that move the business.
