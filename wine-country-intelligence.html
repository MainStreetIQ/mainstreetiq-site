What it is

Every winery in the county, scored and tracked

The Wine Country Intelligence Report is a 25 to 40 page branded PDF, published quarterly. Each issue covers every tracked winery in a single county, scores them across eight dimensions, and shows how each one moved since the last issue.

Santa Barbara County

Published now. 175+ wineries tracked across Santa Ynez Valley, Sta. Rita Hills, Los Olivos District, Ballard Canyon, Happy Canyon, Santa Maria Valley, and Alisos Canyon.

San Luis Obispo County

Launching July 2026. 200+ wineries across Paso Robles' Rhone and Bordeaux varieties and Edna Valley's cool-climate Chardonnay and Pinot.

Why it exists

Most wineries have no idea where they stand against the producers down the road on the surfaces a buyer actually checks. The report makes that visible, every quarter, with the math behind it.

The methodology

Eight dimensions, scored the same way every quarter

Every tracked winery is scored on the same eight dimensions, so movement is comparable issue to issue and winery to winery.

Online visibility

How findable the winery is across classical search, answer engines, and generative AI.

Review authority

Volume, recency, rating, and platform spread of third-party reviews.

Brand consistency

Whether name, story, and visual identity hold together across every surface.

Web presence

Technical health, speed, mobile experience, and structured-data hygiene of the site.

Customer experience

How clearly the visit, the club, and the buying paths are explained and supported.

Social signals

Activity, cadence, and engagement across the platforms that matter for the region.

Conversion flow

How cleanly a visitor moves from interest to club signup, reservation, or order.

Offering depth

How completely the wines, the club tiers, and the experiences are presented online.

In every issue

What each quarter's report surfaces

Top movers

The operators who gained and lost the most ground since the last issue, named, with the dimensions that drove the move. The clearest read on who is investing and who is drifting.

AVA spotlight

One appellation gets a deep look each issue: how the cohort scores, where the whole AVA is strong, and where an entire sub-region is leaving attention on the table.

AI visibility leaderboard

When a visitor asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Gemini which wineries to visit, a short list comes back. The leaderboard shows who is on it for the region, and who is not.

Quarter-over-quarter movement

Every tracked winery's trajectory, not just the leaders. Scored the same way each quarter, so you can see your own line and the lines of the wineries you measure yourself against.
