Why this matters now

Discovery moved, and most businesses did not

A prospect used to find you through a ranked page of links and a map of pins. They compared, they clicked, they decided. That funnel is still there, but a new one now sits in front of it: the buyer asks an AI what to buy and who to trust, reads one synthesized answer, and often stops there.

That answer is not built from your website alone. The model assembles it from your reviews, your directory listings, the articles that mention you, the schema on your pages, and the wider citation footprint that surrounds your name. If those signals are thin or stale, you are absent from the answer no matter how good your product is. Your competitor is not winning because they are better; they are winning because they are legible to the machine and you are not.

Discoverability is the work of becoming legible again: surfacing every touchpoint a prospect can use to find you, fixing the ones that are hurting you, and keeping them current as the models change. Done once, it fades. Managed, it compounds.

How the engine works

Surface, optimize, manage

Three moves that turn a scattered, invisible footprint into a managed one that keeps working while you run the business.

1. Surface every touchpoint

We map the full set of places a prospect can discover you and score each one. You leave knowing exactly where you show up, where you are absent, and which gaps are costing you the most, instead of guessing which channel to chase this quarter.

2. Optimize what actually moves prospects

Not every fix is worth making. We sequence the work by what actually pulls prospects toward you, from the schema and pages you control to the citation footprint you influence, so effort lands where it compounds rather than where it is convenient.

3. Manage it as it drifts

The models change, competitors move, and a footprint left alone goes stale. Monitor is the outside check that the work is still working: it re-reads your visibility every month and flags the drift, so the gains you made in month one still hold in month twelve.

The outcome we are after is simple: more of the prospects already searching for what you sell end up at your door. How far that moves depends on your starting point and your own execution; we measure it against your real numbers rather than promise a figure.

Common questions

What owners ask about AI discoverability

AI discoverability is whether your business shows up when a prospect asks an AI tool who to buy from. When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for the best option in your category and region, the model names a short list. AI discoverability is the work of getting your business onto that list: finding every touchpoint the answer is built from, from classical search and reviews to your external citation footprint, then optimizing and managing it so the models cite you.

SEO optimizes for a ranked page of blue links a person scrolls. AI discoverability optimizes for a single synthesized answer a model hands back, where there is no page two. The signals overlap but they are not the same: the model builds its answer from your external citation footprint, not just your own pages, so the lever that moves AI visibility usually lives off your website. We read both layers and the classical-search layer underneath them.
