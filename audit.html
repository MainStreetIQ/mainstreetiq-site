Who This Is For

If You Are Here, You Already Know Something Is Off

Most operators who land on this page have one of five situations. The Audit is built for these specifically; if your situation is different, an Intro Call is the right first step.

Your traffic looks fine but your bookings, club signups, or order volume do not

The dashboards show pageviews holding up. The downstream numbers are softer than they were a year ago. Something between the visit and the conversion is changing, and the existing analytics setup cannot tell you what.

You typed your business into ChatGPT or Perplexity and the answer was wrong, generic, or missing

A competitor came up first. Or three competitors came up and you were not on the list. Or the description was three years out of date. AI search is a real channel now, and the way your site reads to an LLM is not the same as the way it reads to a human.

You have a long list of vendor recommendations and no way to sequence them

Your SEO consultant says one thing. Your web agency says another. A new AI visibility tool just emailed you with a third. You need someone outside the vendor stack to look at all of it and tell you what is actually worth doing first.

You are about to spend on a website rebuild and want a baseline first

A rebuild without a documented before-state is a coin flip. The Audit is the before-state your project plan, your agency contract, and your board update can all reference.

You want a written document, not a subscription seat

You are not looking to log into another platform. You want a real piece of work you can read on a plane, forward to your partner, and hand to your web person with three things circled. That is what this is.

Why This Is Not a Subscription

What a Dashboard Cannot Do

There is a new category of AI visibility platform that will sell you a monthly seat to watch your brand mentions in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. Some of them are good at what they do. None of them are a substitute for a written diagnosis, because watching the number move is a different job from deciding what to do about it.

What a subscription dashboard gives you in 90 days

A live feed of prompt responses across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and a few others. Brand mention counts. Sentiment trend lines. Competitor share-of-voice. Credits consumed. An interface to log into. Often, a Slack alert when the number drops. Most platforms in this category, the Profounds and Otterlys and Peecs of the world, charge between roughly $100 and $500 per month at the meaningful tier. After 90 days you will have seen the data move three times, and you will still have to decide what, if anything, to do.

What a single Audit Pro gives you

A document that names what is happening, why it is happening, the three or four findings that matter most for the next two quarters, the specific recommended changes for each one, what your web person should ship this month, what to skip, and what would change if you were running a winery or a wellness clinic or an ecommerce brand rather than a generic business. A baseline you can hand to your partner, your agency, your board, or your lender. No login required, no recurring credit, no platform to maintain.

When the dashboard is the right answer

If your job is tracking AI visibility as an ongoing KPI for a marketing team that already knows what to do, a dashboard is the right shape. We will tell you that on an Intro Call if it applies. The Audit is the wrong product for a buyer who needs continuous monitoring of a known optimization program.

When the Audit Pro is the right answer

If you are an owner-operator deciding what your next two-quarter web and AI plan should be, and you do not have a marketing director whose full-time job is to interpret a dashboard, the Audit Pro is the right shape. It compresses the work a strategist would do over a six-week engagement into a single document delivered in a week.

Vertical Depth

Four Verticals, Four Different Query Packs

The audit harness is the same. The query packs, the commentary, and the buyer framing are different per vertical. Generic SEO audits treat your business as a website. The Main Street IQ audit treats it as the category it actually is.

Wine

Wine-drinker query packs (tasting room visit intent, varietal recommendation, regional discovery, club gifting). Commerce7, vinSUITE, and VineSpring-aware structured-data checks. DTC, wholesale, and tasting room channel framing. Wine club retention and acquisition lens on every finding.

Health & Wellness

Wellness-buyer query packs (membership intent, service comparison, modality research, local discovery). Service-business schema checks. Membership vs single-visit funnel framing. Compliance flags on any finding that touches health claims.

Elective Medicine

Patient-decision query packs (procedure research, provider comparison, recovery and outcome questions, regional discovery). Provider and practice-schema checks. Multi-modality framing for practices spanning surgery, injectables, skin, or cosmetic dental. Compliance flags on any finding that touches medical claims.

Ecommerce

Buyer-intent query packs (category research, brand recommendation, comparison shopping, marketplace alternatives). Shopify and Amazon-aware structured-data checks. Multi-channel framing for DTC, marketplace, wholesale, and TikTok Shop. Unit-economics lens on every finding (what does this lift cost to capture, what would the CAC implication be).
