Engage and Manage

Two Shapes of the Same Relationship

Advise is the deepest involvement the practice offers, and it comes in two shapes. Engage is this page; Manage has its own. Here is the pair side by side.

Engage

Senior CFO judgment on a standing cadence, with Monitor Plus running underneath. A monthly strategic-oversight session and a quarterly review with a refreshed roadmap. You keep your finance team and you own the books; we bring the judgment to pricing, hiring, capital, and channel decisions.

Manage

We run the finance function outright. The close, the cash, the books, the reporting, and the dashboard as recurring work, with the CFO embedded weekly. Everything in Engage sits inside it.

The line between them: who owns the books. Engage advises while your team executes; Manage takes the function. Both are scoped on an Intro Call rather than priced off a page, because the shape depends on what you already have in-house. Capital raises, M&A, lender packages, and succession events sit outside both, and neither replaces your CPA or provides legal, tax, or investment advice.
