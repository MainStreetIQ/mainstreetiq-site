Whether your strategy is working

We measure from the outside, you get the report card

The recurring relationship, and the checks and balances on your strategy: a trusted third party measuring whether it is working, from the outside and against the competition, with the same instruments as Diagnose pointed at change over time. Bookings and sales are lagging indicators; if the dimensions on the card are not improving, you can be relatively certain the business is not improving either. This is where the practice becomes ongoing, and the structural bridge into the room.

Monitor

The monthly report card on your own surface: search and AI visibility, reputation, discoverability, and the metrics tied to your next decision, scored the same way every month. Single-business view; the leading indicators that move before revenue does, so drift is caught while it is still cheap to correct.

Monitor Plus

Adds the cohort overlay, extending the check to the competition. You against four named competitors, scored on the same dimensions you are scored on, every month, so a strong month in isolation cannot hide lost ground. Plus specifically means cohort; additional named peers beyond the four are priced separately.

Where these stop: Monitor reads and reports; it does not put the CFO in the room or act on the findings. When the cadence keeps surfacing decisions you would rather not make alone, that is Engage.
